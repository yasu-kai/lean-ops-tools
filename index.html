How we review software

Our goal is not to publish the longest list. It is to help a specific business understand what will actually fit its workflow.

Define the use case

We start with the business problem, team size and workflow rather than the product category alone.

Evaluate relevant options

We compare pricing, usability, core capabilities, limitations and fit for small teams.

Test where practical

We use hands-on evaluation, product documentation and verifiable sources whenever possible.

Explain the trade-offs

We make recommendations by scenario so readers can choose the right tool, not simply the most popular one.

“Small businesses do not need more software. They need the right software for the way they actually work.”
